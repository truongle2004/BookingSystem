### 9.7 Explicitly deferred from the MVP

These items are intentionally not part of the initial MVP, but several are now planned in Sections 13-16 instead of being permanently deferred.

| Item | Status / reason |
|---|---|
| Edit or delete review, owner reply to review | Deferred until a moderation policy exists |
| Account deletion and data export | Deferred until retention/privacy rules are defined |
| Early checkout with partial stay settlement | Deferred; normal checkout/completion remains supported |
| Public rate limiting and abuse protection | Required before internet-facing production, but not assigned to the current milestone |
| Flights, car rental, airport taxi, attractions | Out of product scope; this project is accommodation-only |
